PRIVACY
POLICY.

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Calibr Media LLC ("Calibr Media," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and how you can request its deletion.

This policy covers two distinct groups:

  • Clients — dealerships and businesses that hire us to manage their advertising.
  • Consumers — individuals who submit a lead form or interact with an advertisement we manage on behalf of a client.

1. Information We Collect From Clients

When you engage Calibr Media as a client, we collect:

  • Business contact information (name, email, phone, business name, address)
  • Advertising account and Facebook Page access, when granted by you
  • Vehicle or service inventory data from your website or inventory feed
  • Campaign performance data and analytics
  • Billing information, processed by Stripe (we do not store full payment card numbers)
  • Communications between you and Calibr Media

2. Information We Collect From Consumers (Lead Data)

When you submit a lead form on a Facebook or Instagram advertisement managed by Calibr Media on behalf of one of our clients, we receive the information you voluntarily provided in that form. Depending on how the form is configured, this may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal code or city
  • Vehicle or service of interest
  • Answers to custom questions configured by the advertising client (for example, preferred contact time, trade-in status, or financing interest)

We receive this information from Meta Platforms, Inc. through the Facebook Lead Ads API, using the leads_retrieval and pages_manage_leads permissions granted to us by the client who owns the Facebook Page.

We collect only the fields shown on the lead form at the time you submit it. We do not collect information about you from your Facebook or Instagram profile beyond what you enter into the form and what Meta pre-fills for you, which you can review and edit before submitting.

How Lead Data Is Used

Lead data is used for exactly one purpose: delivering your inquiry to the business you contacted so they can respond to you.

Specifically, we:

  • Retrieve the lead from Meta in real time via a secure webhook
  • Store it in our access-controlled database
  • Display it in the client's private dashboard so their sales team can follow up
  • Where the client has configured it, forward it to that client's CRM or notify them by email

What We Do Not Do With Lead Data

  • We do not sell lead data to anyone, under any circumstances.
  • We do not share lead data with any party other than the specific client whose advertisement you responded to.
  • We do not use lead data to build advertising audiences, train models, or market our own services.
  • We do not combine lead data across clients or use it for any purpose beyond the single inquiry you submitted.

Who Controls Your Lead Data

The advertising client — the dealership or business whose advertisement you responded to — is the controller of your lead data. Calibr Media acts as a service provider processing that data on their behalf and under their instructions. Requests to access or delete your data may be directed to either party; see Section 7.

3. Meta Platform Data

Data we receive through Meta's APIs ("Platform Data") is used solely to provide advertising management services to the client who granted us access. We handle Platform Data in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. We do not transfer Platform Data to any data broker, ad network, or party unrelated to the service we provide to that client.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell any information. We share information only as follows:

Recipient What is shared Purpose
Meta Platforms, Inc. Campaign, ad, and targeting configuration Delivering advertisements
The advertising client Lead data from their own campaigns only Responding to consumer inquiries
Stripe, Inc. Client billing information Payment processing
Twilio SendGrid Email addresses for notifications Transactional email delivery
Render (hosting) Data at rest in our application database Infrastructure

Service providers are contractually restricted to using data only to perform services for us.

We may also disclose information where required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

5. Data Retention

Data type Retention period
Consumer lead data 24 months from submission, then permanently deleted
Client account and contact data Duration of the engagement plus 24 months
Campaign performance and analytics Duration of the engagement plus 24 months
Billing records 7 years, as required for tax and accounting purposes

When a client relationship ends, we delete or return all lead data associated with that client within 30 days of written request.

6. Data Security

We protect information using:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) for all data transmitted to and from our systems
  • Encrypted storage at rest in a managed PostgreSQL database
  • Access controls limiting lead data visibility to the client who owns it and authorized Calibr Media personnel
  • Credential rotation and secrets stored in environment configuration, never in source code

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable and industry-standard measures to protect the data entrusted to us.

7. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

Regardless of where you live, you may request that we:

  • Confirm what information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information
  • Stop processing your information
  • Revoke advertising account or Page access granted to us (clients)

To make a request, email wesconk@calibrmedia.com with the subject line "Data Request."

Include the email address or phone number you submitted so we can locate your record. We respond within 30 days.

Full deletion instructions are available at calibrmedia.com/data-deletion.

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt out of sale — noting that we do not sell personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to businesses and adult consumers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our services after a change constitutes acceptance.
